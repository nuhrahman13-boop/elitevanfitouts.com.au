Good to know

About Elite
FAQ.

Straight answers about our Melbourne workshop, custom fitouts and after-installation support.

Where is Elite Van Fitouts based?

Our workshop is at 8/8 Enterprise Drive, Rowville VIC 3178. We serve tradies across south-east Melbourne and greater Victoria.

What van fitout services do you offer?

We design and install custom van drawers, shelving, false floors, slide-out storage, ply lining, flooring and custom ply cargo partitions.

Are your van fitouts custom made?

Yes. We plan around the vehicle, tools, stock and daily workflow, then CNC-cut the components for a precise, repeatable fit.

What does the one-year warranty cover?

Our installations include a one-year workmanship warranty. If you notice a problem with our work, contact us and bring the vehicle back for inspection. We will explain what is covered and address eligible workmanship issues.

Can I combine storage and protection?

Yes. Drawers and shelving can be planned with ply lining, flooring and a ply cargo partition. Use the shared build tracker to carry selections between the Fitouts and Protection pages.
