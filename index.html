04. The Build Process

1. CAD Scan & Design
We laser-scan your specific van model to achieve exact factory-contour tolerances before a single panel is cut.
2. CNC Precision Fabrication
All marine-grade structural plywood and aluminum profiles are precision machined on industrial CNC routers.
3. Modular Assembly & Quality Check
Components are pre-assembled and rigorously stress-tested for load rating, rattle resistance, and smooth drawer action.
4. Professional Installation
Your custom system is securely bolted into factory mounting points by our specialist team in Melbourne.
