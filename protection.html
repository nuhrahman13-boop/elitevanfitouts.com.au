Good to know

Protection FAQ

Can I combine all three options?

Yes. Ply lining, flooring and a ply cargo partition can be selected together. Each component can have its own permitted finish.

Why is rubber flooring-only?

Rubber is designed as a durable, high-grip horizontal load surface. For walls and ply partitions, Hex Ply or carpet provides the cleaner and more practical finish.

Can this be added before shelving and drawers?

Yes. Installing the protective shell first creates a clean foundation for a complete storage fitout. Your saved tracker carries both page selections together.

Is the ply partition an ADR-certified barrier?

A custom ply partition and a certified safety cargo barrier are not automatically the same product. Tell us your load-restraint or compliance requirements so the correct solution can be specified in your quote.
